Community Guidelines

Blend'n is built for real-world connection, not chaos. These guidelines help keep the platform respectful, safe, and enjoyable for everyone.

Blend'n is designed to help people connect in the moment at events and hotspots. That only works when people feel safe, respected, and in control of their experience.

By using Blend'n, you agree to follow these Community Guidelines along with our Terms of Service. If you violate these rules, we may remove content, restrict features, suspend your account, or permanently ban you from the platform.

1. Respect the vibe

Blend'n is a shared social space. Be respectful, intentional, and mindful of the people around you.

You should:

  • Communicate respectfully
  • Treat others with dignity
  • Use the platform in good faith
  • Respect boundaries and consent

2. Anonymity is not immunity

Some features on Blend'n allow pseudonymous participation. You may be anonymous to other users, but not to the platform.

We maintain internal accountability for moderation, safety, abuse prevention, and legal compliance. Misusing anonymity to harass, deceive, threaten, or harm others is strictly prohibited.

3. What is not allowed

Harassment and hate

Do not post or send content that includes harassment, threats, bullying, intimidation, hate speech, slurs, or discriminatory behavior.

Privacy violations

Do not attempt to identify other users without their consent. Do not share someone else's personal information, contact details, or private content.

Sexual misconduct and explicit content

Do not send unsolicited sexual messages, sexually explicit content, or inappropriate advances. Any exploitative or abusive sexual behavior is prohibited.

Violence and harmful behavior

Do not threaten violence, encourage dangerous conduct, promote self-harm, or share graphic violent content.

Spam and disruption

Do not flood chats, send repetitive messages, post irrelevant promotions, or disrupt conversations and event spaces.

Impersonation and deception

Do not impersonate another person, organizer, venue, or brand. Do not misrepresent your identity, affiliations, or intentions.

Illegal or unsafe activity

Do not use Blend'n to facilitate unlawful conduct, scams, stalking, exploitation, or unsafe in-person interactions.

4. Real-world safety

Blend'n supports real-world interaction, so your safety matters both on and off the platform.

Please use common sense:

  • Meet in public places when appropriate
  • Trust your instincts
  • Leave a situation if it feels unsafe
  • Avoid sharing sensitive personal details too quickly

Blend'n does not guarantee the conduct, identity, or intentions of other users.

5. Chatroom behavior

Event and hotspot chatrooms are community spaces. Keep your participation relevant and respectful.

In shared chatrooms, do not:

  • Spam or flood messages
  • Derail conversations with abuse or baiting
  • Post explicit, hateful, or threatening content
  • Use the space for unauthorized selling or promotion

6. Reporting and blocking

If you experience or witness behavior that violates these guidelines, use the in-app reporting and blocking tools.

You can:

  • Report messages
  • Report users
  • Block users
  • Mute conversations where available

We review reports and may take action based on our policies and moderation systems.

7. Moderation and enforcement

Blend'n uses a combination of automated systems and human review to help keep the platform safe.

Violations may result in:

  • Content removal
  • Message hiding
  • Temporary mute
  • Restricted access to features
  • Account suspension
  • Permanent account ban

Serious or repeated violations may lead to immediate enforcement action without prior warning.

8. Community Guidelines are enforceable

These guidelines are part of how Blend'n works. By using the platform, you agree to follow them.

Violating these rules may also violate our Terms of Service.

If you have questions about these guidelines or want to report a serious concern, contact us at:
